🛒 1. Restore Purchases

My purchase didn't apply

If you made a purchase but didn't receive your coins or remove ads, try the Restore Purchases button found in the app's Settings or Store screen. This re-syncs your purchase history with the App Store.

I switched to a new device

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used when you originally purchased, then tap Restore Purchases.

If restoring still doesn't work, email us at papagamesstudio@gmail.com with your Apple ID (email only — never your password) and a screenshot or order number from your purchase receipt.

🎮 2. Lost Game Progress

Progress disappeared after an update

Game progress is saved locally on your device. In rare cases an update may reset local data. If this happens, please contact us with your device model and app version — we'll do our best to assist.

Progress not carried over to a new device

At this time, cloud save is not supported. Progress cannot be transferred between devices automatically. We're working on adding this feature in a future update.

🐛 3. Reporting a Bug

Found something that doesn't look right? We want to know! When reporting a bug, please include:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version (e.g., iOS 18.2)
  • App version (visible in Settings → About)
  • A short description of what happened and how to reproduce it
  • A screenshot or screen recording if possible

Send your report to papagamesstudio@gmail.com and we'll investigate promptly.

📢 4. Ads & Notifications

Removing ads

You can remove ads by purchasing the Remove Ads option in the in-game store. This is a one-time purchase and applies to the current device. Use Restore Purchases on any other device logged in to the same Apple ID.

Disabling personalized ads

To opt out of personalized advertising, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track". You'll still see ads, but they won't be tailored to your interests.

Push notifications

You can disable push notifications at any time via Settings → Notifications → [App Name] on your device.

🗑️ 5. Deleting Your Data

Because we don't collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal profile to delete. Anonymized analytics data is tied to your resettable Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

To effectively de-link all historical data from your device, reset your Advertising Identifier:
iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Reset Advertising Identifier

To request deletion of any data associated with your Advertising ID, contact us at papagamesstudio@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
