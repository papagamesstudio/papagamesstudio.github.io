🌾 1. About the Game

Meadowmark is a mobile farming game by Papa Games Studio. You grow crops and build up a homestead, raid other players' farms, take revenge on those who raided yours, add friends and join a village.

You can play straight away as a guest. Your farm is saved on our game server so that it is still there the next time you open the game.

✉️ 2. PapaGames ID & Login Emails

A PapaGames ID is an optional account that lets you keep your farm when you change or lose your device. It is an email address and nothing else: there is no password.

When you type your email address into the game to create or log in to a PapaGames ID, we send a 6-digit login code to that address. The code works for 10 minutes. The email comes from no-reply@papagamesstudio.com with the subject "123456 is your PapaGames ID code".

  • We send this email only when someone asks for a code in the game with that address.
  • We send no newsletters, promotions or any other marketing email.
  • If you received a code you did not ask for, someone typed your address by mistake. You can ignore the email; without the code nothing happens.

📋 3. Privacy: What Meadowmark Stores

This is a summary for Meadowmark. The full text is in the Meadowmark privacy policy.

WhatWhy
Your farm and gameplay: buildings, crops, raids, battles, friends, village, the farm name you choseTo run the game and keep your progress
A random sign-in token for each device (stored hashed) and the device modelTo let that device open your farm
Your email address, only if you create a PapaGames IDTo send login codes and recognise your account
IP addresses of login and login-code requestsTo limit abuse and protect accounts

We do not sell this information, and we do not use your email address for anything other than PapaGames ID login codes.

🗑️ 4. Deleting Your Farm or PapaGames ID

You can delete your farm from inside the game. A deleted farm, or a deleted PapaGames ID, can be restored by support for 30 days; after that it is erased from our server.

You can also ask us to delete your farm, your PapaGames ID or both by writing to papagamesstudio@gmail.com from the email address of your PapaGames ID, or with your farm's player tag. We will respond within 30 days.
