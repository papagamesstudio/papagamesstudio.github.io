📋 1. Information We Collect

Meadowmark is an online game. You can play straight away as a guest, without giving us your name or email address. Your farm is saved on our game server so that it is still there the next time you play and so that other players can visit and raid it.

Data Type Description Purpose
Farm & Gameplay Data Your farm and progress, the in-game actions you take, raids and battles, friends, village membership, leaderboard results Running the game and keeping your progress
Names You Choose Your farm name (and earlier farm names), your dog's name, and the name of any village you create Showing your farm to other players (see Section 4)
Device Sign-In A player number and a random sign-in token created by our server (the token is stored only in hashed form), your device model and platform (iOS or Android) Letting your device open your farm without a password
Time Zone Your device's time-zone offset from UTC, saved with each battle Showing battle times correctly
Email Address Only if you choose to create a PapaGames ID Sending login codes and recognising your account
IP Address Recorded when you create a farm, log in, ask for a login code, change your farm name, or delete your farm or PapaGames ID Limiting abuse and protecting accounts
We do NOT collect: your real name, phone number, physical location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID) or payment details. Meadowmark has no ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics and no player-to-player chat.

🎯 2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate the game: save your farm, run raids and battles, friends, villages and leaderboards.
  • Let you keep your farm on a new device through a PapaGames ID.
  • Prevent cheating, spam and abuse, and keep offensive farm names out of the game.
  • Diagnose errors on our server and fix bugs.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond those listed above.

✉️ 3. PapaGames ID & Login Emails

A PapaGames ID is optional. It lets you keep your farm when you change or lose your device. It is an email address with no password: when you enter your address in the game, we email you a 6-digit login code that works for 10 minutes.

The email comes from no-reply@papagamesstudio.com. We send it only when someone asks for a code in the game with that address. We use your email address only to send these login codes: we do not send newsletters or marketing email, and we do not share your address with anyone except the email provider that delivers the code (see Section 6).

If you received a code you did not ask for, someone typed your address by mistake. You can ignore the email; without the code nothing happens.

👀 4. What Other Players Can See

Meadowmark is a multiplayer game, so some of your game information is shown to other players:

  • All players can see your farm name, player tag (for example #A1B2), level and trophies; the raid and battle results you took part in; leaderboard positions; whether you have been active recently; and the defences of your outpost when they scout or raid it.
  • Your friends and village-mates can also visit your farm and see your crops, your dog and its name, your market stall and the stamps in your guestbook.
  • Village names are shown to all players.

Other players never see your email address, your device information or your IP address. Please do not use your real name or other personal information in your farm, dog or village name.

📱 5. Information Kept on Your Device

  • Your sign-in details (player number and sign-in tokens) are stored in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain, or encrypted storage on Android). They are not backed up or synced to other devices.
  • A copy of your save, and your in-game settings, are stored in the app's own storage.
  • Postcards (screenshots you take in the game) are saved on your device only and are never uploaded.
  • If you turn on reminders, the game schedules local notifications on your device. We do not use push notifications and do not collect a notification token.

🗂️ 7. Data Retention

  • Your farm and PapaGames ID are kept for as long as they exist, so that you can keep playing.
  • Deleted farms and PapaGames IDs can be restored by support for 30 days and are then erased from our server.
  • Login codes, and the IP address recorded with them, are deleted within a day.
  • Activity feeds (such as village news and friend activity) are deleted after 30 days.
  • Unused guest farms that were never named or played are erased after a week without use.
  • Security records of account events (such as a farm being created, renamed or deleted, a login, or a PapaGames ID being linked), with the IP address and device model, are kept for security and fraud prevention and may be retained after a farm is deleted.

🗑️ 8. Deleting Your Farm or PapaGames ID

You can delete your data from inside the game: open Settings → Delete account.

  • Delete this village deletes the farm you are playing. It works for every farm, including guest farms, and signs out all devices from that farm at once.
  • Delete my PapaGames ID and ALL its villages (shown when you are logged in to one) deletes your PapaGames ID, your email address and every farm in it.

A deleted farm or PapaGames ID can be restored by support for 30 days; after that it is permanently erased from our server.

You can also ask us to delete your farm, your PapaGames ID or both by writing to papagamesstudio@gmail.com from the email address of your PapaGames ID, or including your farm's player tag.

⚖️ 9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under data protection laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including:

  • Right to know and access: ask what information we hold about your farm or PapaGames ID and get a copy of it.
  • Right to correction: you can change your farm name in the game, or ask us to correct other information.
  • Right to deletion: delete your farm or PapaGames ID in the game, or ask us to (see Section 8).
  • Right to object: you can play without a PapaGames ID, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the game or in your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at papagamesstudio@gmail.com. Write from the email address of your PapaGames ID or include your farm's player tag so that we can find your data. We will respond within 30 days.

🧒 10. Children's Privacy

Meadowmark is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us their email address or other personal information, please contact us at papagamesstudio@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

🔒 11. Security

All communication between the game and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign-in tokens and login codes are stored only in hashed form, and access to our server is restricted. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
